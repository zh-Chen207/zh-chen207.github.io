ABOUT ME

Researching acoustics across multiple scales.

My research focuses on combining advanced ultrasonic measurement techniques with computational imaging and artificial intelligence.

At the macroscopic scale, I investigate laser ultrasonic imaging and inversion methods for near-surface defects, including physics-informed neural networks and deep generative imaging.

At the micro- and nanoscale, I work with ultrafast pump-probe acoustics and time-domain Brillouin scattering for thin-film characterization and material parameter estimation.

My broader interest is translating physical models, signal-processing algorithms and machine learning methods into practical engineering tools for nondestructive evaluation and ultrasonic systems.
